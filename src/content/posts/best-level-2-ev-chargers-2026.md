## The short list

**Best overall — [Tesla Universal Wall Connector](amazon:tesla-universal-wall-connector).** 48 A / 11.5 kW, the best app in the category, and the only integrated NACS + J1772 connector. If any non-Tesla EV will ever use your charger, this is the one. [Full review](/chargers/tesla-universal-wall-connector/).

**Best smart charger — [ChargePoint Home Flex](amazon:chargepoint-home-flex).** The fastest here at 50 A / 12 kW, plug-in or hardwired, and one app for home plus 274,000+ public stations. [Full review](/chargers/chargepoint-home-flex/).

**Best value — [EVIQO Gen 2](amazon:eviqo-gen2).** Full 48 A smart charging, the best weather sealing in the roundup, and ~$400 street pricing. The sleeper pick. [Full review](/chargers/eviqo-gen2/).

**Best budget — [Grizzl-E Classic](amazon:grizzl-e-classic).** A UL-listed tank for ~$300–$450. No app, no fuss, 40 amps of Canadian-built reliability. [Full review](/chargers/grizzl-e-classic/).

## How we ranked them

Power matters, but only to a point — anything 40 A and up refills an EV overnight. We weighted connector future-proofing heavily this year (see our [NACS vs J1772 explainer](/posts/nacs-vs-j1772-explained/)), plus install flexibility, app quality, build/warranty, and price. Scores are research-based, not lab tests — [how we score](/how-we-research/).

## Don't overbuy amperage

48 amps needs a 60-amp breaker and often an electrician's panel work. If your panel is tight, 40 amps on a 50-amp circuit (or the [Emporia Pro](/chargers/emporia-pro/) with load management) gets you 90% of the speed for far less hassle. Check with our [panel capacity checker](/tools/panel-capacity-checker/) before you decide.
