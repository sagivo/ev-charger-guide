## The core trade-off

| | Hardwired | Plug-in (NEMA 14-50) |
|---|---|---|
| Max amperage | 48–50 A | 40 A |
| Install cost | Higher (electrician) | Lower (outlet only) |
| Portability | Stays with the house | Moves with you |
| Looks | Cleanest, no outlet box | Outlet + plug visible |
| Rebate eligibility | Widest | Check — some programs exclude plug-in |

## When hardwired wins

- You want the full 48–50 amps (60-amp circuit).
- The charger mounts far from where an outlet would go.
- Your utility rebate requires it.
- This is your "forever home."

Chargers that are hardwired-only: [Tesla Wall Connector](amazon:tesla-wall-connector-gen3), [Tesla Universal](amazon:tesla-universal-wall-connector), [Grizzl-E Ultimate 48A](amazon:grizzl-e-ultimate-48a), [Wallbox Pulsar Plus 48A](amazon:wallbox-pulsar-plus).

## When plug-in wins

- You're renting or may move within a few years.
- Your panel easily fits a 50-amp breaker but you want to minimize labor.
- 40 amps / ~30 miles per hour is plenty (it is, for overnight charging).
- You already have a 14-50 outlet (RV outlet, welder outlet) — verify it's industrial grade first.

Chargers with plug-in options: [ChargePoint Home Flex](amazon:chargepoint-home-flex), [Emporia Pro](amazon:emporia-pro), [EVIQO Gen 2](amazon:eviqo-gen2), [Lectron V-BOX Pro](amazon:lectron-vbox-pro), [Grizzl-E Classic](amazon:grizzl-e-classic).

## The outlet rule

We repeat it because it matters: **industrial-grade outlet only** — Hubbell HBL9450A or Bryant 9450FR. A $15 dryer outlet under a continuous 40-amp load is the most common failure point in plug-in installs. Budget ~$60–$100 for the part and have the electrician torque the terminals to spec.

## Our take

If you own the home and the panel supports it, hardwire a 48-amp charger and never think about it again. If there's any chance you'll move, or your panel is tight, a 40-amp plug-in on a quality outlet gets you 90% of the experience with far more flexibility. Either way, the [panel capacity checker](/tools/panel-capacity-checker/) comes before the purchase.
