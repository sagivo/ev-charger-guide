Choosing a Level 2 charger comes down to four decisions, in order: your panel, your amperage, your connector, and your install type. Get those right and the brand almost picks itself.

## 1. Start with your panel, not the charger

Your electrical panel decides everything. A 48-amp charger needs a dedicated 60-amp breaker (continuous loads are capped at 80% of breaker rating); 40 amps needs a 50-amp breaker. If your panel is full or only 100-amp service, run our [panel capacity checker](/tools/panel-capacity-checker/) before shopping — you may need the [Emporia Pro](/chargers/emporia-pro/) with load management instead of a panel upgrade.

## 2. Pick your amperage: 40 vs 48

| Charger output | Miles of range per hour* | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| 32 A / 7.7 kW | ~24 | 40-amp breaker |
| 40 A / 9.6 kW | ~30 | 50-amp breaker |
| 48 A / 11.5 kW | ~37–44 | 60-amp breaker, hardwired |
| 50 A / 12 kW | ~38–45 | 60-amp+ breaker, hardwired |

\*At ~3.2 mi/kWh efficiency; your EV's onboard charger is the final cap.

For overnight charging, 40 amps is plenty. Buy 48 if the circuit is cheap to add; don't fund a panel upgrade for the last 8 amps.

## 3. Pick your connector: NACS vs J1772

2026 is the transition year. Teslas and virtually all 2025+ US EVs use NACS natively; older non-Tesla EVs use J1772. Your options: a native-connector charger, a dual-connector charger like the [Tesla Universal Wall Connector](amazon:tesla-universal-wall-connector), or a J1772 charger plus an adapter. Our [NACS vs J1772 explainer](/posts/nacs-vs-j1772-explained/) covers this in depth.

## 4. Pick your install: hardwired vs plug-in

Hardwired unlocks 48–50 amps and looks cleanest, but needs an electrician and stays with the house. A NEMA 14-50 plug-in caps at 40 amps but is cheaper to install and moves with you. Full breakdown: [hardwired vs plug-in](/posts/hardwired-vs-plug-in-ev-charger/).

## 5. Decide how smart it needs to be

Worth paying for: **scheduling** (off-peak rates can halve your charging cost) and **energy tracking**. Nice but optional: solar integration, load management, voice control. Skip the premium if you'll never open the app — the [Grizzl-E Classic](/chargers/grizzl-e-classic/) exists for exactly this buyer.
