## What changed

For a decade, North America had two AC plugs: Tesla's proprietary connector and J1772 for everyone else. Starting with 2025 model-year vehicles, nearly every automaker switched new EVs to Tesla's design — now standardized as SAE J3400, universally called NACS. Your next EV will almost certainly have a NACS port.

## What it means for your charger purchase

A home charger is a 10-year appliance, so buy for the cars you'll own across that span, not just today's:

- **All Teslas / 2025+ EVs:** buy NACS (or dual-connector). Native, no adapters.
- **Older non-Tesla EV, keeping it years:** J1772 is fine and often cheaper — the [Grizzl-E Classic](/chargers/grizzl-e-classic/) is J1772-only and excellent.
- **Mixed household or undecided next car:** the [Tesla Universal Wall Connector](amazon:tesla-universal-wall-connector) with its integrated Magic Dock, or a J1772 charger plus a ~$50–$70 adapter in the frunk.

## Adapters: the cheap bridge

Adapters work fine for AC home charging — this isn't DC fast charging, where adapter quality matters more. A J1772-to-NACS adapter lets any Tesla use a J1772 charger; a NACS-to-J1772 adapter lets older EVs use NACS chargers. Keep the adapter with the car, not the charger.
