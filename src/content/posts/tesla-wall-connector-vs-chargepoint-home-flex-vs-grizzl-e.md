Three brands dominate home EV charging conversations. Here's how they actually compare.

## At a glance

| | Tesla Wall Connector / Universal | ChargePoint Home Flex | Grizzl-E Ultimate 48A / Classic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Max power | 48 A / 11.5 kW | 50 A / 12 kW | 48 A / 11.5 kW (Ultimate); 40 A (Classic) |
| Connector | NACS (std) / NACS+J1772 (Universal) | J1772 or NACS variant | J1772 or NACS (Ultimate); J1772 (Classic) |
| Install | Hardwired only | Plug-in or hardwired | Hardwired (Ultimate); plug-in (Classic) |
| App | Best in class | Great + public network | Good (Ultimate); none (Classic) |
| Warranty | 4 years | 3 years | 3 years |
| Street price | ~$440–$600 | ~$500–$600 | ~$480–$650 (Ultimate); ~$300–$450 (Classic) |

## Tesla: the default for Tesla households

The [Wall Connector](amazon:tesla-wall-connector-gen3) (~$450) and [Universal Wall Connector](amazon:tesla-universal-wall-connector) (~$575) share the best app, power-sharing for up to 6 units, and a 4-year warranty. The Universal's integrated Magic Dock makes it the only charger here that natively handles any EV. Weakness: hardwired only, and non-Tesla app features are thinner.

## ChargePoint: the power and flexibility play

The [Home Flex](amazon:chargepoint-home-flex) is the only one of the three with a plug-in option and the fastest output (50 A). Its killer feature is the network: the same app that schedules your home charging also finds and pays for 274,000+ public stations. Weakness: the highest price of the three for the core unit, and Wi-Fi setup can be fiddly.

## Grizzl-E: the tough value play

The [Ultimate 48A](amazon:grizzl-e-ultimate-48a) matches Tesla's 48 amps with a cast-aluminum NEMA 4X box for less money, plus your choice of connector. The [Classic](amazon:grizzl-e-classic) drops the app entirely and drops the price to ~$350 — the best-built dumb charger you can buy. Weakness: smaller support network, thinner app ecosystem.

## Verdict

- **Tesla household:** Tesla Wall Connector (or Universal if a non-Tesla might ever visit).
- **Mixed EVs / road-tripper:** ChargePoint Home Flex.
- **Toughness and value:** Grizzl-E Ultimate 48A — or the Classic if you don't want an app.
