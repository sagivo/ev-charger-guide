The [Autel MaxiCharger AC Home](amazon:autel-maxicharger-ac-home) is the most feature-dense home charger you can buy: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, *and* Ethernet, a genuinely good app with off-peak optimization, and 50-amp / 12 kW output. Autel has built automotive diagnostics since 2004, and it shows in the hardware. The 50-amp hardwired model runs ~$599; the AC Lite budget line starts at ~$399.

## Who it's for

Tech-forward buyers who want the deepest connectivity and smart-charging stack — including Ethernet for garages with weak Wi-Fi. Value-first buyers should compare the [EVIQO Gen 2](/chargers/eviqo-gen2/).

## Smart features

The Autel Charge app covers scheduling, off-peak/TOU optimization, energy tracking, OTA firmware updates, and optional RFID start/stop. Adaptive load management and charger-to-charger dynamic load balancing (on the 80 A unit) are genuinely advanced. No OCPP/utility-program support was found for the Home line.

## Power and install

50 A / 12 kW hardwired is the fastest in this roundup on paper; the 40 A plug-in version covers renters and simpler installs. Adjustable from 6–50 A. NEMA 4 outdoor rating, CSA tested to UL standards, 3-year warranty.

## How it compares

Versus the [ChargePoint Home Flex](/chargers/chargepoint-home-flex/): same 50-amp class, but the Autel's connectivity (Ethernet!) and app depth win for techies while ChargePoint's public network wins for road-trippers. Versus the [Emporia Pro](/chargers/emporia-pro/): the Emporia's whole-home load management is unique; the Autel's feature list is longer.
