The [ChargePoint Home Flex](amazon:chargepoint-home-flex) is the premium smart-charger pick: the fastest mainstream home charger at 50 amps / 12 kW, your choice of plug-in or hardwired install, and an app that also runs your public charging. At ~$500–$600 it's not cheap, but nothing else combines this output with this ecosystem.

## Who it's for

Drivers who road-trip on the ChargePoint network and want one app for home and public charging, or anyone who wants the fastest home charging available with a plug-in option. Pure value seekers should look at the [EVIQO Gen 2](/chargers/eviqo-gen2/) or [Grizzl-E Classic](/chargers/grizzl-e-classic/).

## Power and install flexibility

50 A / 12 kW is the most power in this roundup — about 37 miles of range per hour on an efficient EV. The plug-in versions (NEMA 14-50 or 6-50) are renter-friendly and avoid hardwire labor; the hardwired version unlocks full output. Our [hardwired vs plug-in guide](/posts/hardwired-vs-plug-in-ev-charger/) walks through the trade-offs.

## The network effect

The differentiator is the network: scheduling, Alexa integration, and energy tracking at home, plus find-and-pay for 274,000+ public stations in the same app. Owner complaints center on fiddly Wi-Fi setup — usually solved by a 2.4 GHz network near the garage.

## How it compares

Versus the [Tesla Wall Connector](/chargers/tesla-wall-connector-gen3/): faster on paper and more install-flexible, but pricier and the Tesla app is better for Tesla owners. Versus the [Autel MaxiCharger](/chargers/autel-maxicharger-ac-home/): similar max power, but the Autel's connectivity stack (Ethernet included) is denser while ChargePoint's public network is unmatched.
