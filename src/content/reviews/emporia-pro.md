The [Emporia Pro](amazon:emporia-pro) is the charger for older homes: its PowerSmart load management can deliver a full 48 amps on a panel that couldn't otherwise support a 48-amp charger, potentially saving thousands on a panel upgrade. At ~$599 it's not the cheapest 48-amp smart charger, but nothing else bundles whole-home energy monitoring this way.

## Who it's for

Homeowners with 100-amp panels or loaded panels who were quoted a panel upgrade. If your panel has room to spare, the [EVIQO Gen 2](/chargers/eviqo-gen2/) gives you 48 amps for ~$200 less.

## Load management, explained

The Vue 3 monitor watches your panel's total draw. The charger then uses only the headroom that's actually free — full 48 amps overnight when the house is quiet, less during the dinner peak. It's the difference between a $599 charger and a $2,500 panel upgrade. Our [panel capacity checker](/tools/panel-capacity-checker/) helps you see where you stand.

## Smart features

Wi-Fi app with scheduling, real-time energy tracking, solar-charging integration (charge from excess solar), and peak-demand management. UL listed, ENERGY STAR certified, NEMA 4 outdoor rating, 3-year warranty. State of Charge named it a Top Home EV Charger of 2025.

## How it compares

Versus the [ChargePoint Home Flex](/chargers/chargepoint-home-flex/): the ChargePoint is faster (50 A) with a better public-charging ecosystem, but can't do whole-home load management. Versus the [Autel MaxiCharger](/chargers/autel-maxicharger-ac-home/): the Autel's connectivity is denser, but only Emporia bundles the panel monitor.
