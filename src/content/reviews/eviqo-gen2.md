The [EVIQO Gen 2](amazon:eviqo-gen2) is the sleeper pick of this roundup: a full 48-amp / 11.5 kW smart charger with the best weather sealing here (NEMA 4 / IP66), standout owner ratings, and street prices around $400. It undercuts the big brands by $150+ while matching them on the specs that matter.

## Who it's for

Value buyers who want 48 amps and an app without paying the Tesla/ChargePoint premium. If the app is your top priority, the [ChargePoint Home Flex](/chargers/chargepoint-home-flex/) or [Autel MaxiCharger](/chargers/autel-maxicharger-ac-home/) are more polished.

## Power and install

48 A / 11.5 kW hardwired on a 60-amp circuit, or 40 A / 9.6 kW as a NEMA 14-50 plug-in. Amperage adjusts in 1-amp increments via the app, plus a hardware DIP switch — the finest granularity in this roundup. The 25-foot cable reaches most parking setups.

## Hardware touches

Reviewers call out genuinely nice details: a rubberized handle with a metal latch, a side reset button, a fluorescent holster, and a steel-shielded mainboard. The enhanced Wi-Fi antenna helps in detached garages. These are the touches you don't expect at $400.

## How it compares

Versus the [Lectron V-BOX Pro](/chargers/lectron-vbox-pro/): similar price and power, but the EVIQO has a longer cable, better weather sealing, and stronger owner ratings. Versus the [Grizzl-E Classic](/chargers/grizzl-e-classic/): ~$80–$130 more gets you 48 amps, an app, and IP66 sealing — worth it for most buyers.
