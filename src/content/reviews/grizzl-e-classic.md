The [Grizzl-E Classic](amazon:grizzl-e-classic) is the value/ruggedness champion: a UL-listed, made-in-Canada, cast-aluminum tank of a charger for ~$300–$450. It's deliberately dumb — no app, no Wi-Fi — and that's the point. You plug it in, set the DIP switches to your circuit, and it charges at up to 40 amps / 9.6 kW for years.

## Who it's for

Buyers who want maximum durability at minimum price and don't need smart features. If you want scheduling and energy stats, step up to the [Grizzl-E Ultimate 48A](/chargers/grizzl-e-ultimate-48a/) or the [EVIQO Gen 2](/chargers/eviqo-gen2/).

## Power and install

40 A / 9.6 kW adds roughly 30 miles of range per hour — plenty for overnight charging of any EV. Plug-in install on a NEMA 14-50 means no hardwire labor; use our [panel capacity checker](/tools/panel-capacity-checker/) to confirm your panel can spare a 50-amp circuit.

## The honest trade-offs

The cable can be stiff in extreme cold, there's no app or power sharing, and it's J1772-only — 2025+ EV owners need to budget for an adapter. None of that matters if you want a charger you install once and forget.

## How it compares

Versus the [EVIQO Gen 2](/chargers/eviqo-gen2/): the EVIQO adds 48 A, an app, and better weather sealing for ~$80 more — the better buy if you want any smarts at all. Versus the [Lectron V-BOX Pro](/chargers/lectron-vbox-pro/): similar price, but the Lectron is a 48 A smart charger with a shorter cable and less rugged build.
