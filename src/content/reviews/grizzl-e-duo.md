The [Grizzl-E Duo](amazon:grizzl-e-duo) solves an expensive problem: two EVs, one 50-amp circuit, no panel upgrade. It plugs into a single NEMA 14-50 outlet and charges two cars at once, automatically balancing its 40-amp pool between them. At ~$799–$999 it's roughly half the cost of two separate chargers plus the avoided electrical work.

## Who it's for

Two-EV households on limited panel capacity. If you have one EV, or your panel can take two 60-amp circuits, two separate 48-amp chargers (like the [EVIQO Gen 2](/chargers/eviqo-gen2/)) will charge faster.

## The math that sells it

Two 40-amp chargers need two 50-amp circuits — often a panel upgrade at $1,500–$3,000. The Duo needs one. The trade-off is a 40-amp total ceiling and zero smart features: no app, no scheduling, no Wi-Fi. Run your numbers in our [panel capacity checker](/tools/panel-capacity-checker/).

## Build and install

Same tank-like NEMA 4 / IP67 cast-aluminum build as the rest of the Grizzl-E line, with two 24-foot premium cables. Plug-in install — no hardwiring, which keeps electrician costs down.

## How it compares

Versus two [Grizzl-E Classics](/chargers/grizzl-e-classic/): the Duo is cheaper than two Classics and needs one circuit instead of two. Versus the [Tesla Wall Connector](/chargers/tesla-wall-connector-gen3/) with power-sharing: Tesla's setup is smarter and faster per car, but costs more and is Tesla-only.
