The [Grizzl-E Ultimate 48A](amazon:grizzl-e-ultimate-48a) is United Chargers' flagship: the brand's tank-like metal build and full smart features in one box, for ~$480–$650 — less than Tesla or ChargePoint charge for the same 48 amps. Add your choice of J1772 or native NACS connector and load-balancing across multiple units, and it's the enthusiast's pick.

## Who it's for

Buyers who want Grizzl-E durability *and* an app, without paying Tesla/ChargePoint prices. If you need a plug-in install, the [Grizzl-E Classic](/chargers/grizzl-e-classic/) or [EVIQO Gen 2](/chargers/eviqo-gen2/) fit better.

## Power and install

Full 48 A / 11.5 kW on a 60-amp breaker, adjustable from 16–48 A. Hardwired only — the hardwire kit (terminal lugs, heat-shrink) is included, but you still need an electrician. Our [installation cost guide](/posts/level-2-ev-charger-installation-cost-rebates/) covers typical pricing.

## Smart features and build

The Grizzl-E Connect app covers scheduling, energy statistics, remote control, and diagnostics, with demand-response/utility program participation. The NEMA 4X / IP67 cast-aluminum enclosure is UL tested — this is a charger you can mount outside in a snowstorm. Reviewer consensus (EnergySage): the go-to for durability-plus-app at a fair price.

## How it compares

Versus the [Tesla Universal Wall Connector](/chargers/tesla-universal-wall-connector/): similar money, tougher box and a native-NACS option, but no integrated dual connector and a thinner app ecosystem. Versus the [Emporia Pro](/chargers/emporia-pro/): the Emporia's whole-home load management is unique; the Grizzl-E's build is tougher.
