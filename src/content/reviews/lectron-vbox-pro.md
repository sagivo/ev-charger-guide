The [Lectron V-BOX Pro](amazon:lectron-vbox-pro) is the design-led budget play: full 48-amp / 11.5 kW smart charging for ~$370–$400, in a good-looking package with a distinctive V-shaped LED status indicator. Lectron made its name on EV adapters, and the V-BOX Pro brings that value ethos to home charging.

## Who it's for

Buyers who park close to the install location and want maximum amps per dollar. If you need a long cable reach, the [EVIQO Gen 2](/chargers/eviqo-gen2/) gives you 25 feet for similar money.

## The cable caveat

16 feet. That's the whole catch, really — measure your parking distance before buying. Everything else is solid: 16/24/32/40/48-amp adjustment, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth app with scheduling and kWh caps, IP65 outdoor rating, and both J1772 and NACS variants.

## How it compares

Versus the [EVIQO Gen 2](/chargers/eviqo-gen2/): the EVIQO costs about the same with a longer cable, better sealing, and stronger ratings — the safer default. Versus the [Grizzl-E Classic](/chargers/grizzl-e-classic/): the Lectron is a 48-amp smart charger for similar money, but the Grizzl-E is far tougher and simpler.
