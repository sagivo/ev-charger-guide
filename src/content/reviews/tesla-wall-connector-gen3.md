The [Tesla Wall Connector](amazon:tesla-wall-connector-gen3) is the benchmark home charger for Tesla households: 48 amps, 11.5 kW, the best charging app in the business, and a 4-year warranty for around $440–$475. It's the highest-rated EV charging station on Amazon for a reason. The only real limitation is the NACS-only connector — fine for Teslas, but J1772 cars need an aftermarket adapter.

## Who it's for

Tesla-only households. If that's you, stop here — nothing else charges a Tesla better for the money. If a non-Tesla EV might ever use the charger, read the [Universal Wall Connector review](/chargers/tesla-universal-wall-connector/) instead.

## Power and install

48 amps on a 60-amp breaker is the practical ceiling of home AC charging — about 44 miles of range per hour. Amperage is adjustable from 12–48 A to match smaller circuits. Install is hardwired only, so budget for an electrician; see our [installation cost guide](/posts/level-2-ev-charger-installation-cost-rebates/).

## App and reliability

Wi-Fi setup, scheduling, remote access, charge stats, and over-the-air updates all run through the Tesla app. Power-sharing across up to six units makes it the default for two-Tesla garages. ENERGY STAR certified with a 4-year warranty — the longest standard warranty in this roundup.

## How it compares

Versus the [Universal Wall Connector](/chargers/tesla-universal-wall-connector/): identical speed and app, ~$100+ cheaper, but NACS-only. Versus the [ChargePoint Home Flex](/chargers/chargepoint-home-flex/): the ChargePoint adds a plug-in option and public-network app, but costs more and can't match the Tesla app's polish for Tesla owners.
