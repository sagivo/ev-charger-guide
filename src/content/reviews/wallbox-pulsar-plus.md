The [Wallbox Pulsar Plus](amazon:wallbox-pulsar-plus) is the compact-premium pick: a 48-amp / 11.5 kW charger the size of a sandwich, with multi-unit power sharing (up to 10 units) that nobody else matches. At ~$650–$800 it's the priciest unit in this roundup — you're paying for the form factor and the smarts.

## Who it's for

Design-conscious homeowners and multi-EV households that value power-sharing intelligence. Bargain hunters get the same 48 amps from the [EVIQO Gen 2](/chargers/eviqo-gen2/) for nearly half the price.

## Smart features

The myWallbox app covers scheduling, energy monitoring, notifications, session history, and remote lock, plus Alexa/Google voice control. The standout is Power Boost load management and power sharing across up to 10 units — the most advanced multi-charger coordination here. Charger-side intelligence even works offline.

## The honest caveats

Price is the big one — $650–$800 is a lot when $400 buys 48 amps elsewhere. The 48-amp model is hardwired only. And while the early app/firmware troubles are fixed, Wallbox's software reputation is still recovering.

## How it compares

Versus the [Autel MaxiCharger](/chargers/autel-maxicharger-ac-home/): the Autel gives you 50 amps and denser connectivity for less money, but can't match the Pulsar's size or 10-unit sharing. Versus the [Tesla Universal Wall Connector](/chargers/tesla-universal-wall-connector/): the Tesla is cheaper with a better app, but it's bigger and caps sharing at 6 units.
